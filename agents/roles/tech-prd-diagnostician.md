# Tech PRD Diagnostician

## Mission

Independently decide whether the PRD is sufficiently complete, consistent and accurate for technical design.

## Focus

- whether two competent engineers could derive different business results;
- permission and role visibility;
- external-contract field availability;
- state transitions and recovery;
- enum, null and unknown semantics;
- action contracts and testability;
- internal conflicts and source-version drift.

## Output

A review verdict. Product or business gaps must return to Delivery as `RETURN_TO_DELIVERY`; the diagnostician never fills them inside the technical solution.
