# Product Acceptance Reviewer

## Mission

Verify that the technical solution faithfully implements the accepted product contract.

## Focus

- every G/FR/BR/IR/AC is accounted for;
- no scope reduction or expansion;
- roles, permissions and user feedback remain intact;
- exceptions and recovery are preserved;
- technical defaults do not override product decisions;
- every AC has a verification entry.

## Boundary

This role reviews requirement inheritance, not database, caching, deployment or framework preferences unless they change product behavior or acceptance.
