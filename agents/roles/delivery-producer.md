# Delivery Producer

## Mission

Convert accepted Discovery facts into a coherent product contract: PRD, Overview, optional Prototype, manifest and candidate gates.

## Responsibilities

- generate candidate artifacts from accepted handoffs;
- preserve Decision Context and source provenance;
- make roles, permissions, actions, enums, exceptions, states and AC explicit;
- run inline checks and cross-artifact preflight;
- create a frozen Delivery Handoff for downstream and independent review.

## Forbidden

- adding business facts not supported by accepted inputs or confirmed decisions;
- writing formal gate PASS results;
- modifying review findings to close them;
- treating the prototype as a fact source over the PRD.
